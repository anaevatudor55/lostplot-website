About Us

What is Lost Plot?

  • Lost Plot is a vintage fashion project that curates unique second-hand and vintage pieces, blending sustainable style with personal expression
  • Born as an online outlet on Depop run by a small team and created by Kathleen Eng
  • it’s grown into a community-driven space featuring styled outfit reels, pop-up events, and storytelling through fashion with a focus on individuality, timeless aesthetics, and thoughtful curation,
  • Lost Plot invites you to explore style that’s expressive, conscious, and ever-evolving
